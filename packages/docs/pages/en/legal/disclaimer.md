## 1. Nature of the Project

NextList is an open-source file list program licensed under **AGPL-3.0**. It provides aggregation, browsing and management for storage services **authorized by the user** (cloud drives, object storage, WebDAV, etc.). The NextList website (docs site and plugin market) presents the project and its documentation; it does not store or distribute any user files.

## 2. Storage Credentials & Data Safety

When using NextList you submit credentials for each storage service (cookies, tokens, account credentials) **to your own deployed instance**. Please understand: credentials are kept in the storage configured by your instance (such as Cloudflare KV), and their safety depends on your deployment and operations. Always access your instance over HTTPS, keep the program up to date, and avoid third-party builds of unknown origin. Losses caused by credential leaks, misconfiguration or a compromised instance are borne by the user. Risk-control measures (rate limiting, account bans, session invalidation) that storage providers apply to third-party access are their own policies and unrelated to this project.
