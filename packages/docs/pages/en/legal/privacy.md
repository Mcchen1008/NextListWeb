**Effective date: September 11, 2026**

This privacy policy explains how the NextList website (including the docs site and the plugin market, together "the site") handles visitor information. By using the site you agree to this policy; if you disagree, please stop using the site.

## Information We Collect

The site follows a data-minimization principle. **You can browse all of its content without registering**, and we do not actively collect your name, email, phone number or other identity information. During normal access, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) automatically records standard request logs (IP address, time, User-Agent, requested page) for security and troubleshooting; these logs are retained and processed by the provider under its own policies.

When you use **GitHub sign-in** on the plugin market, we obtain your GitHub username, numeric ID and avatar URL through GitHub OAuth, solely to identify plugin authors and display avatars. We never read your GitHub email, never access your private repositories, and never call the GitHub API unless you explicitly trigger "refresh my plugins". You may revoke the authorization at any time in GitHub's "Authorized OAuth Apps" settings; the related display data is removed as the cache expires.

## Cookies & Similar Technologies

The site uses a small amount of browser storage (Cookie / localStorage) for essential features: login state, theme preference and reading position. These are never used for cross-site tracking.

**Advertising cookies**: the site serves ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies (including third-party cookies) to serve personalized ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalized ad preferences at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) or opt out of some third-party cookies at [www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).

## Third-party Services

The site uses the following third parties, each processing data under its own privacy policy: **Cloudflare** (hosting, CDN and security, [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/)); **Google AdSense** (advertising, [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)); **GitHub** (code hosting, OAuth sign-in and the Giscus comment system, [privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies)); **giscus.app** (comments based on GitHub Discussions, only invoked when you use the comment feature). Depending on their global infrastructure, these services may transfer data to other regions.

## Deployed Instances

Any NextList **deployment instance** appearing in the documentation is operated by its deployer, not by us. Your own NextList instance (your self-hosted file site) is fully under your control: site data (storage credentials, users, configuration) is kept in your own Cloudflare KV / database, and the NextList program itself does not phone home to the developer's servers.

## Data Security & Your Rights

We take reasonable technical measures (HTTPS everywhere, least privilege, placeholder-ized sensitive values) to protect the information we process. Under regulations such as GDPR / CCPA you may request access, correction or deletion of your personal data — since the site holds almost no identity data, such requests usually go to the third parties listed above. This policy may be updated as features change; significant changes will be published on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
